A note about first launch

The downloads are currently unsigned. On macOS, Control-click the app and choose Open. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway in the SmartScreen prompt. Later updates install from inside Aesthetician.

Release notes and checksums

Not a filter pack

The signal path is the look.

Each treatment models the artifacts of the physical or electronic process that made the original image and sound.

01

Real media behavior

Film grain, halation, gate weave, composite subcarriers, tape tracking, print screens, speaker response, and codec damage behave like their historical sources.

02

Live, adjustable previews

Browse by family or era, compare against the original, tune every parameter, save custom looks, and export several jobs at once.

03

Your media stays yours

Processing runs entirely on your computer. There is no account, upload, telemetry, subscription, or remote rendering service.

Open source

Use the app. Read the engine. Make your own era.

Aesthetician is MIT licensed, with a documented CLI and declarative preset system for repeatable or batch work.
